## How to read this

- **Categories.** `DRIFT`: copies that already disagree. `DUP`: copies that still agree. `BYPASS`: code that reimplements an existing shared helper. `HELPER`: inline logic that should be a named utility. `COMPONENT`: a repeated JSX or hook pattern that should be one shared component or hook.
- **Severity** follows the prompt: HIGH is any `DRIFT` a user can see, and any duplication of physics, unit conversion, file-format reading or writing, or persistence keys; MED is three or more copies, more than about 30 lines, or a component pattern in four or more places; LOW is two small copies that agree.
- **VERIFIED** means the cited lines were re-read and the claim confirmed by a second reader, independently of the agent that raised it (against the Java, for ported math). **REPORTED** means a slice agent read the copies and cited them, but no second reader re-read them. Where the verification changed a claim, the entry says so.
- Ids restart at D1; the previous rounds' ids are cited as "old D*n*" where a finding here is a leftover or a regression of one.

## Rejected

- Coincidental similarity: the `.ork` and `.rkt` readers and writers (each mirrors its own format), the 2D, aft and 3D walkers (different renderers), the RASAero and RockSim unit constants, the DXF `asciiFold` and the PDF `toWinAnsi` (different character sets), `autoLengthXml` against the `.rkt` `flown` policy, and the shoelace in `rasaero/sustainer.ts:33-43` (needs a signed area).
- Costs more than it saves: the `JsonListStore` wrappers (`templateStore`, `launchLocationStore`, `presetStore`, `materialStore`, `motorStore`), the per-type field lists in `customParts.ts` and `motorDb.ts`, `useHoverCursor` and the other one-line hook pairs, SchematicCalipers' mirrored orientations, and hand walks that carry inherited state (`hiddenPartIds`, `nodeContext`, `hasStageBelow`, `collectFinSpans`, `rocketLength`).
- Intended: `EVENT_LABEL` against `EVENT_NAME` (short chart labels against long table labels); `RecoveryBaysPane.tsx:305` sorts newest first; the simple guide re-flies only with a chute; `anonymousFetch.ts`'s `readCapped` runs inside the sandboxed frame and cannot import `fetchProgress`.
- Ported kernel math: `autoRadius`'s `previous` and `next`, the `*FromAssembly` resolvers, `tabPosition` and `rebasePosition` (old D95), and `web/scripts/lib/presetVolume.mjs` against `catalogPatch` (guarded by `catalogPatch.kernel.test.ts`).

3. **File formats:** D15 (desktop part names, then `check:fields`), D16, D17 (pin against desktop-written `.rse` files), D27 and D54. Then D50 and D94 together, keeping the golden `.ork` bytes identical.
